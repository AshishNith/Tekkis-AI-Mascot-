Things to try

  • "Hey Tekkie, tell me a joke about sneakers."
  • "Give me a hint for solving 2x + 6 = 14 — don't tell me the answer."
  • Ask something in Hindi, Arabic or Afrikaans.
  • Start talking while Tekkie is talking: it stops and listens.
  • Drag Tekkie anywhere; it remembers the spot.

What's in this step

  • Procedural 3D character (Three.js): hover, blink, eyes follow your cursor, lip sync, waves hello.
  • Realtime voice via Gemini Live, streamed straight from the browser with a short-lived token.
  • States shown on the antenna: green = listening, yellow = thinking, glow = talking, red = error.
